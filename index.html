Gestures and voice
  • Pinch thumb and index finger, then move: spin it
  • Let go while moving: throw it
  • Pinch with both hands: pull apart to zoom, twist to roll, move together to carry it
  • Close your fist and move: carry it around; let go while moving to throw it
  • No camera: drag with a finger or mouse
  • Say: stop, spin, bigger, smaller, faster, slower, reset, globe, ring, cube, image, deeper, flatter, closer, further, status, time, help, quiet
  • Move it: left, right, up, down, center, corner, or "top left", "bottom right"
  • Build: "add cube", "add sphere", ring, pyramid, cylinder, cone; then next, remove, clear
  • Colour: say red, blue, green, purple, pink, orange, yellow, teal, white, grey or cyan; "paint red" recolours a whole design
  • Image info: say "explain" and the AI tells you what your image shows; "Orbit, ..." questions can be about the image too
  • Style: say "solid" for realistic lit 3D, or "wireframe" for glowing lines
  • Design: city, tower, bridge, arena, house, robot, alien, character; "again" for a new version
  • Say "Orbit" then anything: ask the AI, or ask it to change the hologram
